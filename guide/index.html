Common Mistakes the AI Fixes

Please do the needful and revert back to me.

Please complete the task and get back to me.

Indianism: "do the needful" and "revert back" are common in Indian English but not standard business English.

I am having 5 years of experience in software development.

I have 5 years of experience in software development.

Incorrect continuous tense for permanent states.
